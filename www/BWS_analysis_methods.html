Best-Worst Scaling (BWS) Analysis Methods

Analysis Method BWS Case(s) Use / Purpose
Count Analysis Case 1 & Case 2 Quick descriptive measure of most/least preferred items
Multinomial Logit model (MNL) Case 1 & Case 3 Estimates population-level utilities for items or profiles
Paired Model / Marginal / Marginal Sequential Model Case 2 Captures paired or sequential choices; compares best vs worst; suitable for Marginal and Sequential BWS analyses
Hierarchical Bayesian model (HB) Case 1 Estimates individual-level and population-level utilities; accounts for heterogeneity
Latent Class Analysis (LCA) Case 1 & Case 3 Identifies preference segments / clusters of respondents
