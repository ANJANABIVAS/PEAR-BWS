BWS comes in three variants, each designed for different evaluation contexts and choice set formats.

BWS Case 1 (Object Case): Evaluates a set of standalone items.
Simplest way of BWS, where respondents choose the ‘best’ and ‘worst’ from small sets of items, making preference identification straightforward and effective.

BWS Case 2 (Profile Case): Assesses profiles based on multiple attributes.
Deals with profiles, which presents different combinations of attribute levels, like features of a product or concept. Respondents select the most and least important elements from each profile, helping to understand which attributes matter most.

BWS Case 3 (Multi-Profile Case): Compares multiple profiles within a choice set.
Presents respondents with multiple profiles, asking them to identify the most and least preferred options. By evaluating three or more profiles at once, this method captures nuanced preferences and deeper insights.
